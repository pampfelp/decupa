# Decupa — três conceitos de tela

Para colar numa IA generativa de imagem. Em inglês porque modelo de imagem
acerta mais composição e tipografia em inglês.

Peça **16:9**. Rode cada um **duas ou três vezes**: prompt conceitual varia
muito mais que prompt de wireframe, e a segunda rodada costuma ser melhor que
a primeira.

Estes prompts descrevem **caráter e hierarquia**, não medida. É de propósito.
Pedir "campo com borda de 1px e botão à direita" empurra o modelo pro layout
mais visto no treino dele, que é justamente o jeito de IA do qual o workflow
imagem-primeiro existe pra fugir.

O que é inegociável nos três: o nome **Decupa**, tudo em português, **nenhum
emoji**, e o texto transcrito tratado como leitura, nunca como saída de
terminal.

---

## Conceito 1 — sala de montagem

O nome vem de decupagem, que é o termo de cinema pra transcrever material
bruto. Este conceito leva isso a sério.

```
A bold, striking UI design concept for a web app called "Decupa" that turns a
video link into written text. Present it as a confident design presentation
shot, not a functional wireframe. Interface language is Brazilian Portuguese.

FEELING: a film editing room translated into a quiet modern interface. The word
"decupagem" is the cinema term for transcribing raw footage, and the screen
should carry that heritage without being retro or skeuomorphic.

THE COMPOSITION: an enormous wordmark "Decupa" anchoring the upper left, set in
a confident contemporary serif, large enough to be architecture rather than a
logo. The place where you paste a link is a long horizontal slot that reads as
cut into the surface of the page, not as a floating box. Below, the work in
progress is laid out as a horizontal track with a marker travelling along it,
divided into four named stages in Portuguese: baixando, convertendo,
transcrevendo, pronto. The track should read as a physical strip of tape, with
rhythm and repetition, never as a generic loading bar.

THE HERO: at the lower right, a column of transcribed Portuguese prose set in a
beautiful serif with generous leading, treated with the care of a printed page.

PALETTE: warm paper off-white, near-black ink, and one deep teal used like a
single pass of ink on a press. Flat, matte, no gradients, no glow, no drop
shadows, no purple, no blue, no bright green.

Crisp typography, generous negative space, strong asymmetry, editorial poster
sensibility. No emoji, no stock icons, no photographs of people, no browser
chrome, no device frame, no mouse cursor.
```

## Conceito 2 — a onda virando letra

A ideia inteira do produto numa imagem só: som de um lado, texto do outro.

```
A bold, striking UI design concept for a web app called "Decupa" that turns a
video link into written text. Present it as a confident design presentation
shot, not a functional wireframe. Interface language is Brazilian Portuguese.

FEELING: the moment sound becomes language. High contrast, graphic, almost a
poster.

THE COMPOSITION: the centre of the screen is one continuous horizontal gesture.
It begins on the left as a dense audio waveform and, moving right, the waveform
progressively dissolves and resolves into lines of Portuguese text, so that
sound and writing are the same object caught at different stages. This
transformation is the hero of the screen and occupies most of it.

AROUND IT: a small confident wordmark "Decupa" in the top left. A single narrow
line where a video link is pasted, discreet, sitting quietly above the gesture.
Along the waveform itself, four small marks label the stages in Portuguese:
baixando, convertendo, transcrevendo, pronto, so the progress lives inside the
artwork rather than in a separate bar.

PALETTE: warm neutral off-white, near-black ink, and one deep teal used for the
waveform and the marks. Flat and matte, no gradients, no glow, no neon, no drop
shadows, no purple, no blue, no bright green.

Enormous negative space, confident asymmetry, editorial poster sensibility,
crisp legible typography. No emoji, no stock icons, no photographs of people,
no browser chrome, no device frame, no mouse cursor.
```

## Conceito 3 — a folha

O texto manda em tudo. A interface quase desaparece.

```
A bold, striking UI design concept for a web app called "Decupa" that turns a
video link into written text. Present it as a confident design presentation
shot, not a functional wireframe. Interface language is Brazilian Portuguese.

FEELING: an annotated manuscript. The interface is almost absent; the writing is
everything.

THE COMPOSITION: the entire screen is a single sheet of warm paper. A long
passage of transcribed Portuguese prose dominates it, set in a large beautiful
serif with wide leading and a narrow measure, as carefully typeset as a printed
book page. Everything else is subordinate to it.

IN THE MARGIN: down the left edge, in the narrow margin beside the text, sit
small handwritten-feeling marginal notes, each one a past transcription, each
carrying a tiny four-stage mark labelled in Portuguese: baixando, convertendo,
transcrevendo, pronto. They read as annotations in the margin of a manuscript,
not as a sidebar of cards.

AT THE VERY TOP: one thin discreet line where a video link is pasted, and a
small wordmark "Decupa". Both deliberately quiet, almost hidden, so that
arriving at the screen means arriving at the text.

PALETTE: warm paper off-white with a faint printed texture, near-black ink, and
one deep teal used only for the marginal marks. Flat and matte, no gradients,
no glow, no drop shadows, no purple, no blue, no bright green.

Beautiful typography above all, generous margins, calm and confident. No emoji,
no stock icons, no photographs of people, no browser chrome, no device frame,
no mouse cursor.
```

## O que olhar na hora de escolher

**Julgue a composição, não as letras.** Modelo de imagem embaralha texto longo.
Palavra inventada no parágrafo não é defeito da proposta.

**Ignore cor exata, fonte exata e arredondamento.** Isso se ajusta depois em
minutos, e deixar pesar agora é escolher pelo motivo errado.

As duas perguntas que decidem:

1. **Isso podia ser de outro app qualquer?** Se sim, o conceito falhou. É pra
   isso que serve a regra 14 do design system.
2. **Dá pra imaginar isso funcionando com quatro transcrições na tela?**
   Conceito bonito que só funciona com um item vira problema na semana dois.

**A tensão honesta entre os três:** o 1 e o 2 são mais ousados e mais difíceis
de virar código; o 3 é o mais fácil de construir e o mais fácil de ficar
parecido com qualquer leitor de texto. Não existe escolha errada aqui, existe
escolha com custo diferente, e o custo eu digo depois que você escolher.
